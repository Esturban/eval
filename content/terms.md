Card payments are processed by Stripe. EV Advisory never stores your card details. For fixed-price offers billed by milestone, each invoice is due on the terms stated at the fit call before work on that milestone begins.

## Delivery

Delivery timelines start from scope confirmation, not from payment. Scope confirmation happens on the receipt page or the fit call, whichever the offer uses. If information needed to start the work is late, the delivery clock starts when it arrives.

## Refunds
