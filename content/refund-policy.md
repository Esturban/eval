## How a refund is paid

A refund is issued to the original payment method through Stripe. Where the guarantee waives a milestone payment, that payment is simply not invoiced; no separate refund step is needed.

## Requesting a refund

Reply to your receipt or booking confirmation email. State which guarantee condition applies. EV Advisory confirms and processes the refund within 5 business days of the request.
