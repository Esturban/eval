This is one specific diagnostic among several EV Advisory runs. It checks Shopify or ecommerce revenue reporting, not AI agent fit or vendor governance. If your team keeps asking which number is the real one, run it below.

It is built specifically for Shopify and ecommerce operators who need to understand whether the problem is revenue-signal trust, attribution confidence, conversion friction, reporting drag, or a combination of them.

Looking for something else? The free [Narrow Agent Fit Checklist]({{< relref "get/narrow-agent-fit-checklist" >}}) checks whether a repeated process is narrow enough for an agent, and the free [Live Audit]({{< relref "live-audit" >}}) is a call for anything broader.

You will get:

- an immediate score
- a short diagnosis
- a next step that matches the level of risk
- a follow-up path if the result needs a deeper conversation

This is not a maturity test for vanity metrics. It is a quick way to see whether your revenue signal is strong enough to support confident commercial decisions.
