EV Advisory is built for teams that need proof before they spend the budget, not promises.

The proof pages below are the clearest examples of that work:

- [Reporting Time Compression]({{< relref "insights/resources/case-study-bsg-lighting-fast-reporting" >}}): reduced recurring reporting time from 30 hours per month to 5 minutes, saving more than $4,000 monthly in operating drag. Best evidence for an owner-operator weighing whether a manual, repeated process is worth automating.
- [Lifecycle Revenue Recovery]({{< relref "insights/resources/case-study-harry-rosen-nurturing-clients" >}}): generated more than $100,000 in three months from clients who were already in the ecosystem. Best evidence for a sales or growth leader deciding whether lifecycle automation is worth investing in.

Honest gap: EV Advisory does not yet have a published governance or security case study. If you are a procurement or IT lead evaluating this practice on that basis, say so directly and ask for references instead of taking this page's word for it.

## What these outcomes are meant to show

They are not random wins. Each one reflects the same operating principle:

- improve the quality of the signal or the decision
- reduce the friction around the decision
- make the action easier to defend and easier to repeat
