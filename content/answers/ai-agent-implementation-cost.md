**Most Canadian small businesses should budget about $5,000 to $15,000 CAD to implement a first AI agent as a fixed-price project, plus roughly $300 to $800 a month to run and maintain it.** A single simple workflow can cost under $3,000 to build, while custom multi-agent systems run from $15,000 to $100,000 or more.

The price depends mostly on how many tools the agent connects to, how much judgment the task needs, and whether it touches customer data. The ranges below are from publicly published 2026 Canadian sources, cited in each row.

*Updated 2026-09-24 by Esteban Valencia, EV Advisory. Prices in Canadian dollars unless noted.*

These are vendor-published ranges, not audited industry averages. Treat them as a sense-check on any quote you receive, not as a price list.

## What drives the price up or down?

- **Number of connected tools.** Each integration adds build, testing, and maintenance. One tool is far cheaper than four.
- **Judgment required.** Sorting, summarising, and drafting are cheaper than deciding. Tasks with many exceptions cost more.
- **Customer-facing or internal.** Anything a customer sees unreviewed needs more testing and guardrails.
- **Personal information.** Handling customer data adds safeguards and review. Canada's privacy regulators expect [safeguards and transparency](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/ai-technology-and-innovation/artificial-intelligence/ai_business/) when AI tools use personal information.
- **Clarity of the scope.** A process you can describe in one sentence, with 20 real examples ready, is cheaper to build than one that has to be discovered.
- **Who builds it.** Published Canadian hourly rates run from about $80 an hour for junior contractors to $200 to $350 for senior boutique consultants and $300 to $500 for tier-1 agencies ([rate card](https://chatgpt.ca/blog/ai-consulting-rate-card-canada-2026)).

## What are the ongoing monthly costs?

Budget for three monthly lines after launch:

| Cost | What it covers | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| Model usage | The AI model's per-use fees. Scales with volume | Varies with volume; ask for an estimate at your expected volume |
| Tool subscriptions | Any automation platform or agent tool the agent runs on | Depends on the tools; often already part of your stack |
| Maintenance or managed support | Fixes when connected tools change, small adjustments, monitoring | About $300 to $800 a month for maintenance; about $2,000 to $5,000 a month for managed optimization ([Planwebbies](https://planwebbies.ca/blog/ai-automation-cost-canada/)) |

## How should you budget the first year?

Add it up the simple way: **build cost + (12 × monthly run cost) + a contingency for changes.** For example, using the published ranges above, a $10,000 build with $500 a month in run and maintenance costs comes to $16,000 in year one before contingency. That is an illustration of the arithmetic, not a quote.

Then compare it with what the process costs you today: hours per week, times the loaded hourly cost of the people doing it, times 52. If the agent does not plausibly beat that within 12 months, pick a different first workflow. The [AI agent ROI guide](/answers/measure-ai-agent-roi/) covers how to measure it.
