# Entity: Design Asset

A mockup, screenshot, or rendered draft produced for a design review before it becomes
real Hugo content or a CSS change. Working material, not the shipped page.

## Where it lives today

- Design review drafts: `docs/design/*.html` and `docs/design/*.png`.
- Shipped visual system and brand tokens (once a design asset is approved and becomes
  real code, not a draft): `assets/css/style.css`, `tailwind.config.js`,
  `data/brands.json`.

## Relation to other entities

A design asset is drafted for a [[Entity: Landing Page]] before that page ships. See
[[CRO Ontology]] for the full map.
