# Entity: Lead Magnet

A free, narrowly scoped deliverable (a checklist, a short guide) traded for an email
address or a conversation, meant to start a relationship rather than close a sale.

## Where it lives today

- Delivery page in this repo: `content/get/*/index.md`, for example
  `content/get/narrow-agent-fit-checklist/index.md` and its thank-you page.
- Listed as a free-tier row in `data/store.toml`.
- The lead magnet spec and the sell-by-chat DM ladder that delivers it are owned by a
  different seat and live in a sibling repo, not in eval: `lead-intel/docs/lead-magnet-buyer1.md`
  and `lead-intel/docs/dm-ladder.md`. This wiki points at that spec, it does not copy it.

## Relation to other entities

A lead magnet is offered from a [[Entity: Landing Page]] and is one rung of the
[[Entity: Offer]] ladder. See [[CRO Ontology]] for the full map.
