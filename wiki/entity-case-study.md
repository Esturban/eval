# Entity: Case Study

A documented client outcome used as proof: a named result, a measurable before and after,
and enough detail that a prospect can trust it happened. Used on landing pages and in
outreach to back a claim that would otherwise be unverified.

## Where it lives today

- Summary and index: `content/proof.md`, which lists the strongest outcomes and links out
  to the individual write-ups.
- Individual case studies: `content/insights/resources/case-study-*.md`. Two exist today:
  a reporting-time-compression story and a lifecycle-revenue-recovery story.
- `content/live-audit/_index.md` names the Live Audit call itself as a future case-study
  source, with client sign-off required before anything goes public.

## Relation to other entities

A case study backs an [[Entity: Offer]] on a [[Entity: Landing Page]] and can originate
from an [[Entity: Audit]] call. See [[CRO Ontology]] for the full map.
