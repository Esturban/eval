# Entity: Audit

A diagnostic touchpoint that inspects a prospect's or lead's real tool stack or funnel and
names the first thing worth fixing or automating. Two shapes exist: a live call and a
self-scored form.

## Where it lives today

- Live Audit (the free 20-minute call): `content/live-audit/_index.md`, booked through the
  external `cal.com/este-val/audit-fit-call` link.
- Scorecard (self-scored form): `content/scorecard.md`, backed by
  `data/revenue_signal_scorecard.json`.
- Weekly discoverability audit process (a different, internal kind of audit, of the site
  itself rather than a prospect): `docs/ai-discoverability-ops.md` and
  `docs/weekly-ai-discoverability-scorecard.md`.

## Relation to other entities

An audit can turn into an [[Entity: Case Study]] with the client's permission, and often
sits behind a [[Entity: Landing Page]] as the entry offer. See [[CRO Ontology]] for the
full map.
